# Sales-Performance-Excel-Dashboard

## Project Initiative
This is a project uses a public data set and a simulated business scenario to demonstrate skills in data analysis, reporting, and excel for my portfolio. 

## Business Request

Company stakeholders have requested a report analyzing the sales performance of the business over the past year. The company is considering performance-based expansion into departments with high sales output while providing additional corporate support to underperforming areas.

The analysis is intended to answer the following business questions:

* Which regions generate the most revenue and profit?
* Which product categories generate the highest sales?
* Which sales representatives generate the most profit?
* During which months is the company's profit margin highest?
* How does sales performance change throughout the year?

Using a dataset containing transaction-level information, including Transaction ID, Product ID, Sales Date, Region, Sales Representative, Sales Amount, Quantity Sold, Product Category, and Unit Cost, an interactive Excel dashboard was developed to present the results through clear and easily understandable visualizations.

The dashboard allows stakeholders to interactively filter the analysis by date and examine sales performance across regions, product categories, and sales representatives.

## Dataset
The dataset contains 1,000 sales transactions covering a one-year period.

Key fields include:

- Transaction ID
- Product ID
- Sales Date
- Region
- Sales Representative
- Sales Amount
- Quantity Sold
- Product Category
- Unit Cost
- Discount
- Profit

## Data Preparation
- Condensed data set to only transactions made in 2023
- Sorted data set by Sales Date from newest to oldest
- Added a TransactionID column for an identifier for each transaction
- Created a separate clean data sheet and kept raw data sheet intact

## Analysis
Created Pivot Tables to calculate important calculations both simple and complex.

Simple Calculations include:
-Total Revenue
-Total Profit
-Order Volume(units sold)
-Profit Margin

Complex Calculations include:
-Revenue by region
-Sales by product category
-Profit by Sales Representative
-The aforementioned three calculation by month
-Revenue, Profit, and Profit Margin over time

## Dashboard
An interactive dashboard that visualizes sales performance by month over the last year.

Components inlcude:
-Revenue KPI
-Profit KPI
-Total Orders KPI
-Date Slicer
-Profit by Sales Representative Bar Chart (connected to date slicer)
-Sales by Product Category Bar Chart (connected to date slicer)
-Revenue by Region Bar Chart (connected to date slicer)
-Monthly Revenue, Profit, and Profit Margin Bar and Line Combo Chart

The date slicer allows the stakeholders to filter the KPI cards and connected charts by month to concisely track data performance trends throughout the year.

Totals

-Total Revenue: $4,999,937.22
-Total Profit: $2,526,272.27
-Total Profit: 51%
-Total Units sold: 25,278
Performance figures

-Top Sales Representative: David generated $607,778.52 in profit for the year.
-Top Product Category: Clothing had the highest number of units sold, with 6,911 units
-Lowest Revenue Region: The South region generated $1,151,880.14

Business Considerations
-Sales operations: Review sales performance and identify successful sales practices for department review on sales operations
-Product Investment: Evaluate demand and sales performance for clothing lines when  considering future production
